Welcome

A family farm in Portland, TN

Bottom View Farm is family-owned land for weddings, festivals, reunions, and the moments in between — Southern hospitality, open sky, and photo-ready views at every turn.

Say “I do” in the barn, gather the whole family, or join us for fall cider and hayrides. New dates land first on Facebook.
