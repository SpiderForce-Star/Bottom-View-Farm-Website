Welcome

About the Farm

Welcome to Bottom View Farm — nestled in the heart of Tennessee, we’re a family-owned and operated destination for weddings, festivals, reunions, and special events. Our land is a place for life’s special moments, where Southern hospitality meets open sky.

Whether you are saying “I do” in the barn, gathering the whole family for a reunion, or joining us for fall cider and hayrides, you’ll find warm welcome and photo-ready views at every turn. Facebook is where we share new dates and day-of memories first — follow along there.
