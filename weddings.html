Your Options

Wedding & Event Spaces

Choose the setting that fits your vision — from a full barn celebration to an intimate lakeside vow exchange.

The Barn Wedding

Our signature celebration space — rustic beams, open air, and room for family and friends to gather for ceremony and reception in true Southern farmhouse style.

The Pond Intimate Wedding

A serene waterside setting for couples who want quiet beauty and a close circle of loved ones — soft light, reflective views, and unforgettable photos.

The Pavilion Intimate Wedding

Covered elegance with a breeze — ideal for an intimate ceremony or cocktail hour with a timeless outdoor feel, rain or shine.

The Point Intimate Wedding

A scenic overlook on the property — panoramic farm views and golden-hour magic for couples seeking a dramatic, personal backdrop.

The Gathering Room

A warm indoor space for dining, dancing, or welcoming guests — perfect as a reception hall or a cozy extension of your outdoor celebration.

The Smokehouse

Character-filled charm with rustic Southern soul — a unique corner of the farm for smaller gatherings, photo moments, or specialty experiences.

The Chapel

A heartfelt place to exchange vows — intimate, meaningful, and steeped in the spirit of family and community that defines Bottom View Farm.

Venues for about 30, 75, or up to 300 guests. Bridal house, groomsmen room, catering & DJ available.
